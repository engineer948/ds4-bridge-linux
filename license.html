5. Games, DRM & anti-cheat

DS4 Bridge only reads standard input events from your controller and passes them to a virtual controller through the Linux kernel. It does not modify games and does not bypass DRM, copy protection or anti-cheat systems.

Some online games restrict the use of virtual input devices or input remapping. It is your responsibility to check and follow the rules of the games and services you use.

6. Changes to your system

DS4 Bridge runs as your normal user. It only changes system settings when you explicitly allow it: the optional permission fix installs a udev rule for /dev/uinput and asks for your administrator password first. "Start on Login" creates a standard autostart entry in your home folder, which you can turn off at any time.

7. Privacy

The DS4 Bridge application does not collect, store or transmit any personal data and does not connect to the internet. Settings and logs are stored only on your computer.
